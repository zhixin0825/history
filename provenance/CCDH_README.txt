CHINESE DYNASTIC HISTORIES: README

This README contains a list of the "Chinese Dynastic Histories OSF Storage (United States)" site files with short descriptions. For main corpus statistics and dynastic histories time periods, see  24_ref_dynasties_new.xlsx file in _24_reference_information folder.

If you use this data resource for research
purposes, please acknowledge by citing the following reference in
your published work:
     
Zinin, S. and Xu, Y. (2020) Corpus of Chinese dynastic histories:
Gender analysis over two millennia. In Proceedings of the 12th
International Conference on Language Resources and Evaluation,
Marseille, France.


The site has 5 main folders.

1) _24_results_files

	24_01_diachronic_context_sent_cutoff.txt 
	- absolute diachronic statistics for sentence context-target
	24_01_diachronic_context_sent_norm_cutoff.txt 
	- normalized diachronic statistics for context-target pairs with a minimum cutoff number of entries for sentence spaces
	24_01_diachronic_context_sent_norm_cutoff_posneg.txt 
	- diachronic normalized context-target pairs for sentences that demonstrate evolution, with a minimum cutoff number of entries
	24_01_diachronic_context_sent_norm_posneg_list.txt
	- diachronic context-target pairs for sentences that demonstrate evolution
	24_02_diachronic_context_par_cutoff.txt
	- absolute diachronic statistics for paragraph context-target
	24_02_diachronic_context_par_norm_cutoff.txt
	- normalized diachronic statistics for context-target pairs with a minimum cutoff number of entries for paragraph spaces
	24_02_diachronic_context_par_norm_cutoff_posneg.txt
	- diachronic normalized context-target pairs for paragraph that demonstrate evolution, with a minimum cutoff number of entries
	24_02_diachronic_context_par_norm_list_posneg.txt
	- diachronic context-target pairs for pargraph that demonstrate evolution
	24_03_context_term_matrix_sent.txt 
	- context-target co-occurence matrix for sentences
	24_04_context_term_matrix_par.txt
	- context-target co-occurence matrix for paragraphs
	24_06_keyword_sent_CHI2f_nǚ.txt
	- statistics and Chi Square scores of context terms of nv target term for sentences
	24_06_keyword_sent_CHI2m_nan.txt
	- statistics and Chi Square scores of context terms of nan target term for sentences
	24_06_keyword_sent_G2f_nǚ.txt
	- statistics and log-likelihood  score of context terms of nv target term for sentences
	24_06_keyword_sent_G2m_nan.txt
	- statistics and log-likelihood  score of context terms of nan target term for sentences
	24_07_keyword_par_CHI2_f_nǚ.txt
	- statistics and Chi Square scores of context terms of nv target term for paragraphs
	24_07_keyword_par_CHI2_m_nan.txt
	- statistics and Chi Square scores of context terms of nan target term for paragraphs
	24_08_keyword_sent_diachron_G2f_nǚ_sent.txt
	- keywords diachronic stats for nv based on sentence GF2
	24_08_keyword_sent_diachron_G2m_nan_sent.txt
	- keywords diachronic stats for nan based on sentence GF2
	24_09_keyword_sent_diachron_G2f_nǚ.txt
	24_09_keyword_sent_diachron_G2m_nan.txt
	24_09_LDA_f_nǚ_sent.txt 
	- nv topics for  LDA
	24_09_LDA_m_nan_sent.txt 
	- nan topics for  LDA
	24_09_LSI_f_nǚ_sent.txt
	- nv topics for  LSI
	24_09_LSI_m_nan_sent.txt
	- nan topics for  LSI
	24_XX_context_chars_freq_stats_sent.txt
	- diachronic statistics of contexts of gender-specific terms
	24_XX_par_m_f_stats.txt
	- statistics for male.female paragraphs
	24_XX_target_terms_entry_stats_.txt 
	- diachronic statistics of gender-specific terms
